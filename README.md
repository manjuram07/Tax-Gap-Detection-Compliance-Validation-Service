Tax Gap Detection & Compliance Validation Service
📌 Overview

Tax Gap Detection & Compliance Validation Service is a Spring Boot microservices-based backend system designed to help tax auditors validate financial transactions, calculate expected taxes, identify tax gaps, execute configurable compliance rules, manage exceptions, maintain audit trails, and generate tax compliance reports.

The system is designed with a focus on modularity, database-driven business rules, financial accuracy, auditability, and SQL-based reporting.

## Services
## Transaction Service – Receives and validates batches of financial transactions and stores transaction information.
## Tax Calculation Service – Calculates expected tax, tax gaps, and compliance status.
## Rule Engine Service – Executes configurable tax compliance rules stored in the database.
## Exception Service – Creates and manages compliance exceptions generated by rules and tax calculations.
## Audit Service – Maintains an audit trail for transaction ingestion, tax computation, and rule execution.
## Reporting Service – Generates customer tax summaries and exception summary reports using SQL aggregation.
